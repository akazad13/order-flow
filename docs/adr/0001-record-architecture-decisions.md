# 0001. Record architecture decisions

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-14

## Context

This is a learning project. Decisions made in week 1 will be questioned in week 6, and the reasoning will be forgotten.

## Decision

Record every significant decision as a short ADR in `docs/adr/`, numbered, with Context, Decision and Consequences. Specs say *what*; ADRs say *why this way*.

## Consequences

- A plan's "Risks and decisions" section links to an ADR when a choice outlives one feature.
- Superseded ADRs stay, marked "Superseded by NNNN".
